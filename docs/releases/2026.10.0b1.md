## Change

The BrewZilla coordinator now uses a maximum idle polling interval of
30 seconds so BrewZilla-backed BLE/control-device temperature is observed
more frequently in Home Assistant.

- the normal three-minute integration setting becomes 30-second polling for BrewZilla;
- operator settings faster than 30 seconds are preserved;
- Pill and other RAPT coordinators keep their configured cadence;
- existing profile/session STOP-attestation semantics are unchanged.

This improves Home Assistant observation frequency only. It cannot force
RAPT Cloud or BrewZilla to publish a fresh `controlDeviceTemperature` every
30 seconds. Repeated identical cloud payloads remain identical in Home Assistant.

## Compatibility

No Home Assistant entity IDs or BrewAssistant discovery contracts change.
BrewAssistant itself does not require a code update solely for this polling
change.
