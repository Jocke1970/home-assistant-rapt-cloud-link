## Change

Adds BrewZilla telemetry freshness diagnostics so stale BLE/control-device
temperature can be traced to Home Assistant polling or upstream RAPT telemetry.

The BrewZilla debug sensor now exposes:

- `telemetry_frequency`
- `last_activity_time`
- `root_modified_on`
- `root_created_on`
- `telemetry_created_on`

No entity IDs or BrewAssistant discovery/control contracts change.
